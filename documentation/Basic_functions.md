# Getting Started

## Bringup
1. Red ON/OFF button next to the screen between the antennae.
2. Green/silver button on the robot at the opposite side of the screen.
3. Wait for the robot to boot – orange lights appear on its side.

## Turnoff
- (from ssh connection) $sudo poweroff
- Use the red button.

## Charging
1. Plug in.
2. Plug out cautiously, pressing the small silver button at the top.

> **CAUTION:** The robot has a lead battery which is capable of running out of power even without switching it on. Furthermore, it damages itself when it is out of power, so **CHARGE AS OFTEN AS POSSIBLE**.

## Teleoperation, Joystick
1. Unplug the power if it's charging.
2. Press `start` on the controller.
3. Push the upper right trigger while teleoperating.
